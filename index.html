Mental relaxation

Conversation replay reset

  1. Name it: “My mind is replaying that conversation.”
  2. Check its job: Is there a specific decision or action I can take, or am I repeating the same scene?
  3. Capture one useful step: Write a message draft, question, boundary, or reminder for tomorrow.
  4. Park it: Tell yourself, “I have saved the useful part. I do not have to solve the rest tonight.”
  5. Redirect gently: Notice three physical sensations, then return to an easy exhale. Thoughts may remain. You do not need to fight them.

Worry parking lot

Racing-thought grounding

Look around and quietly notice: 5 things you see, 4 sensations you feel, 3 sounds, 2 scents or neutral details, and 1 kind next step. If lists feel busy, choose one color and slowly find five objects with that color.

Nighttime relaxation

10–12 minute quiet-down sequence

Designed for when your body feels tired but your mind remains awake. The player combines body softening, comfortable 4-second inhales and 6-second exhales, thought redirection, and quiet rest.

If you remain awake and frustrated, you can get out of bed briefly, do something quiet in dim lighting, and return when sleepy. Keep the goal as rest rather than forcing sleep.
